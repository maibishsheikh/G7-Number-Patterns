# Story Art Brief — PatternQuest

No illustrated artwork has been produced yet for the Story phase. Each of the 4 panels
currently renders a CSS-framed placeholder (`StoryPhase.jsx` → `StoryImage`) — a gradient
background with an emoji and title — so a real illustration can be dropped in later
**without touching any component code**. `storyContent.js`'s `imageBg` field is the
gradient used behind each placeholder today; an illustrator's final art should aim to
work well behind (or replace) that same warm/cool tone per panel.

Target dimensions: **1376 × 768px** (matches the reference architecture's story-image frame).

## Panel 1 — "The Missing Markers"
Farhan, Mei Lin, and Tally the Owl stand at the start of a misty mountain trail. A row of
numbered wooden trail posts (3, 7, 11, 15) recedes into fog; the posts beyond are broken,
leaning, or missing entirely. Overcast, cool grey-blue palette. Sense of a long trail
ahead and an unsolved problem.

## Panel 2 — "Naming the Rule"
Tally the Owl perches on a signpost mid-explanation, one wing gesturing between two trail
markers to show the "+4" gap. Farhan looks eager; Mei Lin looks thoughtful. Warmer forest
green palette, daylight breaking through the canopy.

## Panel 3 — "The Shortcut Formula"
A rustic map table (a log stump or wooden crate) with a hand-drawn expedition map pinned
down, chalk or charcoal scribbles of "4n + 3" crossed out and "4n − 1" written beside it.
Mei Lin points at the number "1" on the map, Farhan mid-realization. Teal palette, focused/
studious mood.

## Panel 4 — "Finding Marker Fifty"
A wide mountain vista at golden hour. Tally the Owl in flight ahead of the pair, landing
beside a weathered post reading "199". Farhan and Mei Lin look triumphant, trail continuing
up toward a distant peak. Deep moss-green to gold gradient, sense of triumph and
forward motion.

## Character reference
- **Farhan** — the scout: quick to spot patterns, occasionally jumps to a rule too fast
- **Mei Lin** — the methodical one: always double-checks a rule against the sequence
- **Tally the Owl** — mascot & mentor: sharp-eyed, first to notice when a pattern shifts
